Our Craft

From Our Pasture to Your Home

Petal & Pasture began with a simple idea: what if the softest things about country life — roses in bloom and the animals of the pasture — could live together in a single candle?

Every cow, pig, and duck is sculpted rose by rose in our signature beeswax blend, paired with a natural bamboo wick for a clean, slow, even burn. Each animal is hand-poured and hand-finished in small batches right here in Texas.
