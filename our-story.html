Our Story

Where the Pasture Meets the Flame

Petal & Pasture began with a simple idea: what if the softest things about country life — roses in bloom and the animals of the pasture — could live together in a single candle?

Sculpted by Hand

Rose by Rose, Batch by Batch

Every cow, pig, duck, and retriever is sculpted rose by rose in our signature premium wax blend, then paired with a natural bamboo wick for a clean, slow, even burn.

Nothing here is mass-produced. Each animal is hand-poured and hand-finished in small batches in the heart of Texas — which means the one that arrives at your door is truly its own little work of art.
